Travel Tips for India

Essential advice for a smooth, safe, and truly enjoyable trip across India.

India is an incredible destination, but it can feel overwhelming for first-time visitors. These practical tips will help you travel comfortably, respectfully, and safely — so you can focus on the amazing experiences that await you.

🎒

Packing Essentials

  • Light cotton clothes for hot and humid weather
  • Warm layers and a jacket for hill stations and winters
  • Comfortable, easy-to-remove footwear (temples require it)
  • A lightweight scarf or dupatta (for temples and mosques)
  • Sunscreen, insect repellent, and hand sanitiser
  • Basic first-aid kit and any personal medications
  • Universal power adapter (India uses Type C/D/M plugs)
💰

Budget Travel Tips

  • Use Indian Railways — affordable, extensive, and scenic
  • Book trains and accommodations weeks in advance online
  • Eat street food and local dhabas for authentic meals at low cost
  • Bargain politely in local markets (it is expected and normal)
  • Use government-run tourist buses and state transport
  • Download the UPI apps for cashless payments everywhere
  • Visit free government museums and temples
🛡️

Safety Tips

  • Use only official prepaid taxis or Ola/Uber app cabs
  • Beware of common tourist scams (fake guides, overpriced shops)
  • Keep photocopies of your passport and important documents
  • Save local emergency numbers: Police 100, Ambulance 108
  • Avoid drinking tap water; use sealed bottled water
  • Do not leave luggage unattended in public spaces
  • Share your itinerary with someone trusted at home
🙏

Cultural Etiquette

  • Greet locals with a polite "Namaste" (hands folded)
  • Remove footwear before entering homes, temples, and mosques
  • Dress modestly when visiting religious sites
  • Ask permission before photographing people or ceremonies
  • Accept food or gifts with the right hand or both hands
  • Avoid public displays of affection in conservative areas
  • Be patient — things often run on "Indian Stretchable Time"!
📅

Best Time to Visit

  • October to March is ideal for most of India (pleasant weather)
  • June to September brings monsoons — lush but disrupted travel
  • Leh-Ladakh is best visited between June and September only
  • Goa and Kerala are perfect from November to February
  • Rajasthan is best explored in winter (Oct–Feb)
  • Avoid peak summer (April–June) in plains — very hot
  • Book during major festivals for unique cultural experiences
🍲

Health & Food Tips

  • Start with milder dishes before trying very spicy food
  • Eat freshly cooked street food at busy, high-turnover stalls
  • Drink bottled or filtered water always — avoid ice in drinks
  • Carry oral rehydration salts and antidiarrheals
  • Consult a doctor about vaccines before travelling (Hepatitis A/B)
  • Wash hands frequently or use sanitiser before eating
  • Vegetarian options are plentiful and delicious everywhere
🚆

Getting Around

  • Indian Railways connects almost every major city — book on IRCTC
  • Domestic flights are affordable; book early for best fares
  • Auto-rickshaws are perfect for short city trips
  • Agree on a fare before boarding an auto-rickshaw
  • Metro systems exist in Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad
  • Renting a scooter is fun in Goa and hill stations
  • Overnight sleeper trains save accommodation costs
💬

Language & Communication

  • Hindi and English are widely spoken and understood
  • Each state has its own regional language — locals appreciate effort
  • Download Google Translate for offline regional language help
  • Buy a local SIM card (Airtel/Jio) for cheap data and calls
  • Internet connectivity is excellent in cities; patchy in hills
  • Learn a few words: "Dhanyavaad" (thank you), "Kitna hua?" (How much?)
  • Locals are very helpful — do not hesitate to ask for directions
