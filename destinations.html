Top Indian Destinations

Explore must-visit places with local tips, cultural insights, and the best experiences.

Jaipur

Rajasthan

Known as the Pink City, Jaipur is a royal gateway to Rajasthan. Its majestic forts, colourful bazaars, and warm-hued architecture make it one of India's most photographed cities. The city blends Mughal and Rajput styles beautifully, giving every corner a regal character.

🗓 Best Time

October – March

🏛 Top Attractions

  • Amber Fort
  • Hawa Mahal
  • City Palace

🍽 Local Food

  • Dal Baati Churma
  • Ghewar
  • Pyaaz Kachori

Goa

Goa

India's smallest state packs an enormous punch — palm-fringed beaches, centuries-old Portuguese churches, vibrant markets, and a unique fusion cuisine. Goa is perfect for beach lovers, history buffs, and party-goers alike. Its laid-back energy is contagious.

🗓 Best Time

November – February

🏛 Top Attractions

  • Baga Beach
  • Dudhsagar Waterfalls
  • Basilica of Bom Jesus

🍽 Local Food

  • Goan Fish Curry
  • Bebinca
  • Patal Bhaji

Varanasi

Uttar Pradesh

One of the world's oldest continually inhabited cities, Varanasi sits on the banks of the holy Ganges. Its ancient ghats, evening Aarti ceremonies, and winding old-city lanes create a profound spiritual atmosphere that moves even the most seasoned travellers.

🗓 Best Time

October – March

🏛 Top Attractions

  • Kashi Vishwanath Temple
  • Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh
  • Sarnath

🍽 Local Food

  • Kachori Sabzi
  • Malaiyyo
  • Baati Chokha

Mysore

Karnataka

Mysore, the City of Palaces, enchants visitors with its magnificent Mysore Palace illuminated at night, fragrant sandalwood products, and the grandeur of the Dasara festival. It is also the yoga capital of India and a gateway to southern culture.

🗓 Best Time

October – February

🏛 Top Attractions

  • Mysore Palace
  • Chamundi Hill
  • Brindavan Gardens

🍽 Local Food

  • Mysore Pak
  • Mysore Masala Dosa
  • Obbattu

Kerala

Kerala — God's Own Country

Kerala's lush green landscapes, tranquil backwaters, and Ayurvedic wellness retreats make it one of the most serene destinations in all of Asia. Glide on a traditional houseboat through the Alleppey backwaters and watch the world slow down beautifully.

🗓 Best Time

September – March

🏛 Top Attractions

  • Alleppey Backwaters
  • Munnar Tea Gardens
  • Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary

🍽 Local Food

  • Kerala Fish Curry
  • Appam & Stew
  • Puttu & Kadala

Leh-Ladakh

Union Territory of Ladakh

Perched atop the Himalayas, Leh-Ladakh is a landscape of raw, breathtaking beauty — turquoise lakes, barren mountain passes, ancient Buddhist monasteries, and a sky full of stars untouched by city light. It is adventure travel at its most awe-inspiring.

🗓 Best Time

June – September

🏛 Top Attractions

  • Pangong Tso Lake
  • Nubra Valley
  • Thiksey Monastery

🍽 Local Food

  • Thukpa
  • Momos
  • Skyu

Rishikesh

Uttarakhand

Known as the Yoga Capital of the World, Rishikesh sits at the foothills of the Himalayas along the sacred Ganges. It offers white-water rafting, spiritual ashrams, and the iconic Laxman Jhula bridge — a perfect blend of adventure and inner peace.

🗓 Best Time

February – May & Sep – Nov

🏛 Top Attractions

  • Laxman Jhula
  • Triveni Ghat
  • Neelkanth Temple

🍽 Local Food

  • Aloo Ke Gutke
  • Bal Mithai
  • Kafuli
