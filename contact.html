Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions we receive from travellers.

Do I need a visa to visit India?

Yes, most foreign nationals require a visa. India offers an easy e-Visa facility for citizens of over 160 countries, which can be applied for online before departure.

Is India safe for solo travellers?

India is generally safe for solo travellers, including solo women. Like any destination, common-sense precautions — staying alert, using reputable transport, and sharing your itinerary — go a long way.

What is the best duration for a trip to India?

A minimum of 2 weeks is recommended to experience even a slice of India meaningfully. A month allows you to cover multiple regions without feeling rushed.

What currency does India use?

India uses the Indian Rupee (₹ / INR). ATMs are widely available in cities. UPI-based digital payments (via apps like PhonePe and Google Pay) are very commonly accepted everywhere.
